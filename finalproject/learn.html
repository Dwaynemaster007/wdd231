Read the market responsibly

Price alone can mislead. These plain-language definitions help you use CryptoPulse data with context.

Market capitalization

Market cap is the coin’s current price multiplied by how many coins are in circulation. A coin at $1 with a billion coins in supply can be larger than a $100 coin with a small supply.

Comparing market caps is often more useful than comparing raw prices when you want a sense of overall size.

24-hour volume

Volume is how much of the asset was traded over the last day (in USD on CryptoPulse). Higher volume usually means it is easier to buy or sell without moving the price as much.

A sharp price move on very low volume is often less meaningful than a move backed by heavy trading.

24-hour change

The percentage change shows how far the price moved compared with about 24 hours ago. Green is up; red is down.

Short-term swings are common in crypto. Use change as a signal to look closer, not as a complete investment thesis.

Why price alone is not enough

Two coins can share a similar price while differing hugely in market cap and liquidity. Always check size (market cap) and activity (volume) alongside the price and its daily change.
